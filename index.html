Lunira

A calmer way to manage your money.

Budgeting that never makes you feel bad about a coffee.

Lunira is a lifestyle budgeting app for iOS. You track what comes in and what goes out, set monthly budgets by category, and save toward the things you actually want — with a companion that tells you where you stand instead of grading you for it.

Everything stays on your device.

There is no account to create and no server to sign in to. Lunira has no backend at all: your name, your income, your spending and your goals are written to your phone's local storage and never leave it. We don't collect analytics, we don't run ads, and there is nothing for us to see even if we wanted to.
