Privacy

Nothing leaves your phone.

Lunira has no backend. No account, no sign-in, no sync, no analytics, no ads, no trackers. Your name, your income, your spending and your goals are written to your iPhone’s local storage and stay there.

Which cuts both ways, so it’s worth saying plainly: we can’t read your data, and we can’t get it back for you. Deleting the app deletes everything. Read the privacy policy.
