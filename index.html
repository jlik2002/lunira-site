Money apps love to raise their voice. This one doesn’t.

Lunira is a budgeting app for iPhone. It tells you where you stand in plain words — no exclamation marks, no streak to break, no lecture at the end of the month — and then it stops talking.

The difference

The same $90, said two ways.

One number, one month, one category over plan. The difference is entirely in how it gets said to you — and that difference is the product.

Lunira

That’s it. No exclamation mark, no broken streak, no “let’s do better”. $90 over plan, and the two days you have left to decide what to do about it.

What you actually see

Numbers, and what they mean. In words.

Colour is never the only signal. Every state also says what it means in plain language, so nothing depends on your being able to tell rose from red.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your phone.

Lunira has no backend. No account, no sign-in, no sync, no analytics, no ads, no trackers. Your name, your income, your spending and your goals are written to your iPhone’s local storage and stay there.

Which cuts both ways, so it’s worth saying plainly: we can’t read your data, and we can’t get it back for you. Deleting the app deletes everything. Read the privacy policy.
