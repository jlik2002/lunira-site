Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

Lunira does not collect, transmit, or store your personal information on any server. There is no account, no backend, and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device.

This policy explains that in full, and covers the few places where another company — Apple — is involved.

What Lunira stores, and where

When you use Lunira you may enter a first name, your income, your expenses and their categories, monthly budgets, and savings goals. All of it is written to your iPhone's local app storage.

That data is never uploaded. It is not sent to us, to any third party, or to any cloud service. We have no ability to read it, recover it, or restore it. Two consequences worth knowing:

  • If you delete Lunira, your data is permanently gone. Deleting the app is how you delete your data.
  • A new phone starts empty. There is no sync and no backup, so nothing carries over unless it is included in your own device backup.

What we collect

Nothing. Lunira contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter, and no tracking of any kind. It does not build a profile of you, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Lunira schedules them locally on your device. There is no push server and no device token is registered anywhere, so we cannot send you anything and cannot tell whether you opened a reminder. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

Subscriptions and payments

Lunira's paid plans are sold through the App Store. Apple handles the entire transaction: your payment method, billing address, and purchase history go to Apple, not to us. We never see or store your payment details.

Apple's handling of that information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Lunira is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Since the app collects nothing from anyone, there is no such information to remove — but if you have a concern, please write to us.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export, and delete the personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to hand over or erase. Your data is already entirely under your own control, on your own device: you can view and edit it in the app, and remove it by deleting the app or by using "Delete all data" in Settings.

Changes to this policy

If Lunira ever starts collecting data — for example if optional cloud backup is added — this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and the date at the top will change. Material changes will be announced in the app.
